Privacy policy

frogswamp nutrition is used only by its author, with the author's own Google account.

  • It asks Google only for permission to add nutrition entries to Google Health, and to change or delete the entries it added. It does not read any Google data.
  • The entries come from the author's own meal log. The Google login token is kept encrypted on the author's home server.
  • No data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for anything else.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
